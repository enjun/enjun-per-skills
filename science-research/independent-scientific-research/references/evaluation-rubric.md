# Solution Evaluation Rubric

Detailed scoring criteria for the Evaluator role in independent-scientific-research skill.

## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. Theoretical Soundness (25 points)

**Excellent (23-25)**:
- Solid theoretical foundation with well-established principles
- All assumptions clearly stated and justified
- Mathematical analysis is rigorous and correct
- Theoretical guarantees provided where applicable

**Good (20-22)**:
- Strong theoretical foundation
- Most assumptions justified
- Mathematical analysis is sound
- Minor theoretical gaps

**Fair (15-19)**:
- Adequate theoretical basis
- Some assumptions unexamined
- Mathematical analysis has minor issues
- Limited theoretical justification

**Poor (0-14)**:
- Weak or incorrect theoretical foundation
- Critical assumptions not addressed
- Mathematical errors present
- No theoretical basis provided

### 2. Literature Support (20 points)

**Excellent (18-20)**:
- Strong support from recent, high-quality Zotero papers
- Citations directly relevant to the proposed solution
- Multiple independent sources validating approach
- State-of-the-art clearly established

**Good (15-17)**:
- Good literature support
- Relevant citations present
- Adequate coverage of related work
- Some connections to state-of-the-art

**Fair (10-14)**:
- Limited literature support
- Some citations not directly relevant
- Gaps in related work coverage
- Weak connection to state-of-the-art

**Poor (0-9)**:
- Minimal or no literature support
- Citations missing or irrelevant
- No awareness of state-of-the-art
- No literature review

### 3. Practical Feasibility (20 points)

**Excellent (18-20)**:
- Implementation clearly viable
- Resource requirements well understood
- Challenges identified with mitigation strategies
- Scalability and complexity analyzed

**Good (15-17)**:
- Implementation appears feasible
- Resource requirements considered
- Some challenges identified
- Basic complexity analysis

**Fair (10-14)**:
- Implementation feasibility uncertain
- Resource requirements not fully analyzed
- Significant challenges not addressed
- Limited complexity consideration

**Poor (0-9)**:
- Implementation not clearly viable
- Resource requirements unknown
- Major challenges unaddressed
- No feasibility analysis

### 4. Innovation Level (15 points)

**Excellent (14-15)**:
- Novel contributions clearly identified
- Significant advance beyond state-of-the-art
- Creative and original approach
- High potential for impact

**Good (11-13)**:
- Clear contributions present
- Meaningful advance over existing methods
- Original aspects identified
- Moderate potential impact

**Fair (7-10)**:
- Some incremental improvements
- Limited advance over state-of-the-art
- Minor novel aspects
- Low to moderate impact

**Poor (0-6)**:
- No clear innovation
- Replicates existing work
- No novel contributions
- Minimal impact potential

### 5. Validation Plan (10 points)

**Excellent (9-10)**:
- Comprehensive validation strategy
- Appropriate experiments or analysis planned
- Clear success criteria defined
- Reproducibility ensured

**Good (7-8)**:
- Adequate validation approach
- Relevant experiments planned
- Success criteria mostly clear
- Mostly reproducible

**Fair (4-6)**:
- Limited validation plan
- Some appropriate experiments
- Success criteria unclear
- Reproducibility concerns

**Poor (0-3)**:
- No clear validation strategy
- Inadequate or irrelevant experiments
- No success criteria
- Not reproducible

### 6. Completeness (10 points)

**Excellent (9-10)**:
- All problem aspects addressed
- No significant gaps
- Edge cases considered
- Comprehensive solution

**Good (7-8)**:
- Most aspects addressed
- Minor gaps present
- Some edge cases considered
- Mostly complete

**Fair (4-6)**:
- Several aspects unaddressed
- Noticeable gaps
- Edge cases ignored
- Incomplete solution

**Poor (0-3)**:
- Major aspects missing
- Significant gaps
- No consideration of edge cases
- Incomplete solution

## Overall Score Calculation

Sum all dimension scores for total (0-100).

**Passing threshold**: ≥95 points

**Decision categories**:
- **95-100**: Accept - Solution is ready for implementation/publication
- **80-94**: Revise and re-evaluate - Solution has promise but needs refinement
- **<80**: Major revision needed - Solution requires significant changes

## Weakness Impact Classification

When identifying weaknesses, classify by impact:

**High Impact**: Must be addressed for solution to be viable
- Theoretical flaws
- Missing critical literature
- Implementation impossibility
- No validation plan

**Medium Impact**: Should be addressed for quality
- Theoretical gaps
- Limited literature support
- Implementation challenges
- Incomplete validation

**Low Impact**: Nice to address but not critical
- Minor theoretical issues
- Some missing citations
- Minor implementation details
- Limited reproducibility
